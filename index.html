Our definition

What we mean by pressure

A ball is high-pressure when it can change the result and things are going badly for the player facing it. Either one alone is not enough.

      Does it hold up?

      The game changes as the index rises

      The index never looks at what happened on the ball. Yet as it climbs, batters slow down and bowlers leak, which is what real pressure should do to each of them.

      2. Go first for Anshul Kamboj.An Indian seamer who gets better under pressure, for the overs around Archer.
      3. Buy two finishers.One overseas (Tim David by trade, or Omarzai at auction) and one Indian (Naman Dhir).

      How far to trust this

      • The top two batters and top three bowlers are secure. Places 6 to 10 on each list change in roughly half of reshuffles, and the blue bars show it.
      • Pressure performance repeats far less than overall ability. Our ratings lean on each player's full record and add a pressure adjustment the data has to earn.
      • The Royals verdicts are our judgment on squad fit and availability, layered on the ratings. Player status is as of 16 November 2026, one day before retention lists are due.
      • The match file records a successful chase only as a wickets margin, so the narrow-game counts use runs margins and ties.
      • The weights in the pressure index are informed judgment. When we varied them at random, about 7 of each top ten stayed.
